### Impact of Dropout Rates
Dropout is a regularization technique used to prevent overfitting in neural networks. It works by randomly setting a fraction of the layer’s units to zero during training. This study investigates the effect of different dropout rates (0.2, 0.3, and 0.4) on model accuracy, precision, recall, and F1-score, using the MNIST dataset for handwritten digit recognition.
